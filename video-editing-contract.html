Frequently Asked Questions

How many revision rounds should a video editing contract include?

Standard practice is 2-3 rounds of revisions. The first round addresses major structural changes, the second covers fine-tuning, and a third handles final polish. Define what constitutes a revision versus a new edit direction to prevent scope creep.

Who provides the music and sound effects?

Specify whether the editor sources royalty-free music (and whether licensing costs are included in the fee) or whether the client provides licensed music. Using unlicensed music can result in copyright strikes and legal issues, so clarity here is essential.

How should raw footage and project files be handled?

Define how long the editor will retain raw footage and project files after delivery (typically 30-90 days). Specify whether project files (Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects) are included in the deliverables or available for an additional fee.
