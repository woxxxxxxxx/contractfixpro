Frequently Asked Questions

What should a social media contract include?
It should cover platforms managed, number of posts per month, content creation responsibilities, reporting frequency, account access protocols, and monthly retainer fee.
Who owns the social media accounts?
The client always owns their social media accounts. Your contract should clearly state you are managing them on behalf of the client, not claiming ownership.
What is a typical social media management fee?
Rates vary widely: $300–$500/month for small businesses, $1,000–$5,000/month for comprehensive management including content creation and paid ads.
How should I handle negative comments or PR issues?
Define your response policy in the contract. Specify whether you handle community management and crisis communications, and what your escalation process is.
What happens to content if the contract ends?
All content created for the client's accounts should remain the client's property. Your contract should specify a transition period and content handover process.
