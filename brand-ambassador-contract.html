Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a brand ambassador and an influencer contract?

A brand ambassador contract typically involves a longer-term, ongoing relationship where the ambassador represents the brand consistently. An influencer contract is usually project-based for specific campaigns or posts. Brand ambassador agreements often include exclusivity clauses preventing work with competitors.

Should I include social media metrics requirements in the contract?

Yes. Specify minimum posting frequency, required platforms, content approval processes, and performance benchmarks like engagement rates. This protects both parties by setting clear expectations for deliverables and compensation.

How do I handle product seeding in a brand ambassador contract?

Include a section detailing what products or services the brand will provide, whether they must be returned, and any restrictions on how gifted items can be used or discussed. Specify the retail value for tax reporting purposes.

Review before anyone signs

  • Confirm exact post counts, deadlines, platforms, approval time, and reporting evidence.
  • Separate organic reposting from paid ads, editing, whitelisting, and perpetual usage.
  • Make disclosure language visible with each endorsement and appropriate for its format.
  • Check local worker-classification, tax, privacy, publicity-rights, and consumer-protection rules.

This generator creates a planning draft, not legal advice or a guarantee of enforceability. Have qualified counsel review material or cross-border campaigns.

FTC disclosure terms for ambassador campaigns

A free product, discount, payment, employment, family, or other material connection can trigger a disclosure obligation. The disclosure should appear with the endorsement, be hard to miss, use clear language, and match the endorsement's language and format.

Use the FTC's Disclosures 101 guide and the current Endorsement Guides as primary references, then account for any additional laws that apply to the parties or audience.
