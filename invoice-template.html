Frequently Asked Questions

What should a freelance invoice include?
A professional invoice should include your name and contact info, client details, invoice number, invoice date, itemized services, subtotal, any taxes, total amount due, payment terms, and payment method.
What are standard payment terms on an invoice?
Common terms: Net 15 (due in 15 days), Net 30 (due in 30 days), or Due on Receipt. Shorter terms improve cash flow for freelancers.
Can I charge a late fee on unpaid invoices?
Yes. Specify a late fee in your contract and include it on the invoice: typically 1–2% per month on the outstanding balance after the due date.
What is the difference between an invoice and a receipt?
An invoice is a payment request sent before payment. A receipt is proof of payment issued after payment is received.
Should I include my tax ID on the invoice?
If you are VAT registered or required to collect sales tax, yes. In the US, sole proprietors typically use their SSN or EIN on invoices to clients who need to file 1099s.
