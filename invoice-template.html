Frequently Asked Questions

What information must be included on a professional invoice?

A professional invoice should include: your business name and contact info, client details, unique invoice number, issue date, payment due date, itemized list of services with rates, subtotal, applicable taxes, total amount due, and accepted payment methods.

What payment terms should I set on my invoice?

Common payment terms include Net 15 (payment due in 15 days), Net 30 (30 days), or Due on Receipt. For new clients, shorter terms like Net 15 or a deposit requirement reduce payment risk. Always include late payment fee terms to incentivize timely payment.

How do I handle unpaid invoices?

Start with a polite reminder at the due date. Follow up with a formal past-due notice including late fees as specified in your contract. If payment remains outstanding after 60-90 days, consider a final demand letter before pursuing collections or small claims court.
