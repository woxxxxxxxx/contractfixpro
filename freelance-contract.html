Frequently Asked Questions

What payment structure works best for freelance contracts?

A milestone-based structure is recommended: 25-50% upfront deposit, payments at defined milestones, and final payment on delivery. This protects both parties and ensures cash flow for the freelancer while giving the client checkpoints.

How should I handle scope creep in a freelance contract?

Include a change order clause that requires any work beyond the original scope to be documented in writing with adjusted pricing and timelines. This prevents misunderstandings and ensures you are compensated for additional work.

Do I need a separate NDA with my freelance contract?

The freelance contract template includes a basic confidentiality clause. For projects involving sensitive business information, trade secrets, or proprietary technology, consider using a separate, more detailed NDA in addition to the freelance contract.
