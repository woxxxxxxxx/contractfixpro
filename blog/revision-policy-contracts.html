Revision Policy Clauses for Design, Writing, and Video Projects

Set practical revision limits without damaging client relationships.

Set practical revision limits without damaging client relationships. Use this guide to review contract language before a project starts, not after a dispute appears.

What to Confirm First

Start with the parties, scope, deliverables, payment trigger, approval process, ownership terms, confidentiality, and termination rights. Those details decide how the contract behaves when work changes.

Build a Base Case and a Problem Case

Read the agreement once assuming everything goes well, then read it again assuming payment is late, scope changes, files are disputed, or the relationship ends early.

  • Define deliverables in observable language.
  • Attach payment to milestones or acceptance rules.
  • Keep change requests documented in writing.

Common Mistakes

Avoid vague scope, unlimited revisions, unclear IP transfer, missing late-payment language, and confidentiality terms that are too broad to follow in practice.

Note: ContractFixPro is educational and does not provide legal advice. Have important contracts reviewed by a qualified attorney.
