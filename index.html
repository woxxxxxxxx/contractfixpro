About ContractFixPro

ContractFixPro helps freelancers, designers, photographers, and small business owners create legally sound contracts in minutes — completely free. Our templates cover every major contract type, from simple freelance agreements to complex NDAs and consulting contracts.

Each contract is generated instantly in your browser based on your inputs. All contracts include standard legal sections: Scope of Work, Payment Terms, Intellectual Property, Confidentiality, Termination, and Governing Law clauses. No sign-up required, no watermarks, no hidden fees.
