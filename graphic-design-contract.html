Frequently Asked Questions

What is a graphic design contract?
A graphic design contract is an agreement that defines the scope of design work, deliverables, revision rounds, payment terms, and intellectual property ownership between a designer and client.
When does copyright transfer to the client?
Copyright typically transfers to the client upon full payment of all invoices unless the contract specifies otherwise.
How many revision rounds should I include?
Most designers include 2–3 revision rounds. Unlimited revisions lead to scope creep. Specify what constitutes a revision vs. a new project direction.
What file formats should I deliver?
Always deliver final files in the formats the client will actually use: print-ready PDFs, web-optimized PNGs/JPGs, and editable source files (AI, PSD) if requested.
Can I show the work in my portfolio?
Yes, unless the client requests a confidentiality clause. It's standard practice to include portfolio rights in your contract.
